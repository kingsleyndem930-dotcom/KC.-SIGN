KC Sign

Speak or type English and play a reviewed Nigerian Sign Language phrase.

1. Tap Speak or type a short phrase.

2. KC Sign checks the approved NSL phrase library.

3. If the phrase is approved, the matching sign clip plays. Otherwise, the app shows that the phrase is not yet available.

This is an early version. Sign meanings must be reviewed by fluent NSL signers before they are treated as valid translations.
